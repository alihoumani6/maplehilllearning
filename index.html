Parent corner

Questions families ask

What does "gifted" mean here?

These workbooks go a step beyond a typical grade-level worksheet, for kids who are ready for more. They are not a test or an official program.

Which grade should we buy?

Start with your child's current grade. If it feels easy, try the next one up. The pages build step by step, so it is simple to move around.

Are the pages in color?

The covers are full color. The inside pages are black and white, which keeps the price low and makes them easy to photocopy for your own family.

Is there an answer key?

Yes. Every book has a full answer key at the back.

Where do I buy them?

On Amazon. Use any "Find on Amazon" button, or search for Maple Hill Learning Press. New titles are being added over the coming weeks.
