Maple Leap Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026. Maple Leap is published by Maple Hill Learning Press, Ottawa, Ontario, Canada ("we", "us"). This policy explains how the Maple Leap app and maplehilllearning.com handle information. Maple Leap is designed for children in Grades 1–6, and for their parents.

The short version

We do not collect personal information from children. We do not ask for names, email addresses, birthdays, locations or photos. We do not use analytics, advertising identifiers, tracking, or third-party advertising networks.

What stays on your device

A child's nickname, chosen animal, grade, progress, streaks, XP, gems and settings are stored only on your device. We cannot see them. Deleting the app, or using "Delete everything" in the Parent Zone, erases them. Parents can also download this data from the Parent Zone.

Purchases

Maple Leap Plus and Family subscriptions are bought through Apple's App Store and processed by Apple. We use RevenueCat to confirm whether a subscription is active. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID and purchase status, not a child's name or progress. We never receive payment card details. Purchases (subscriptions and optional heart refills) and links that leave the app are protected by a parental gate.

Advertising

Maple Leap contains no advertising and no third-party ad networks.

Children

We comply with applicable children's privacy laws, including COPPA in the United States and PIPEDA in Canada. Because we do not collect personal information from children, there is nothing to share, sell or disclose.

Website

The maplehilllearning.com website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log standard technical information such as IP addresses when pages are loaded, under its own privacy policy.
