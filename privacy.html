Maple Leap Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026. Maple Leap is published by Maple Hill Learning Press, Ottawa, Ontario, Canada ("we", "us"). This policy explains how the Maple Leap app and maplehilllearning.com handle information. Maple Leap is designed for children in Grades 1–6, and for their parents.

The short version

We do not collect personal information from children. Children are never asked for an email address, birthday, location or photo, and never create an account. We do not use analytics, advertising identifiers, tracking, or third-party advertising networks. Parents may optionally create a parent account with their own email address (see below). The only exception is the optional Classroom feature, described below, which a teacher or a family chooses to use.

What stays on your device

A child's nickname, chosen animal, grade, progress, streaks, XP, gems and settings are stored on your device. Unless a parent turns on sync (next section), we cannot see them. Deleting the app, or using "Delete everything" in the Parent Zone, erases them. Parents can also download this data from the Parent Zone.

Parent account (optional)

A parent can sign in inside the Parent Zone with an email address, using a one-time code (no password). We store the parent's email address and the status of their subscription so a plan bought on the website or in the app works on every device. If, and only if, a parent turns on "Sync my kids' progress", we also store each child's nickname, grade, XP and lesson progress so the website and the app match. Parents can turn sync off, sign out, or ask us to delete the account and all synced data at any time by emailing alihoumani6@gmail.com. Accounts are hosted with Supabase in Canada. We do not sell or share this information and do not use it for advertising.

Purchases

In the iPhone and iPad app, Maple Leap Plus and Family subscriptions are bought through Apple's App Store and processed by Apple. On the website, they are bought through Stripe, which receives the parent's payment details and email; we never see card numbers. We use RevenueCat to confirm whether a subscription is active. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID and purchase status, not a child's name or progress. We never receive payment card details. Purchases (subscriptions and optional heart refills) and links that leave the app are protected by a parental gate.

Classrooms (optional)

Teachers can create a class at maplehilllearning.com/teachers using their email address and a one-time code. A teacher receives a 6-letter class code to share with students. A student who joins a class enters the code and a first name only (or nickname). While a student is in a class, the app sends the teacher's dashboard that first name, the student's grade, and learning progress: minutes practised per day, XP, lesson counts, answers correct per subject, streak and brain-game scores. We do not collect student email addresses, last names, birthdays, photos, locations or device identifiers for this feature, and there is no chat. Student records are stored with Supabase and are visible only to the teacher who owns the class. Teachers confirm that they have their school's permission, and parental consent where required, before using Maple Leap with students. A teacher can remove any student or delete a class at any time, which permanently deletes the associated student records. A student (or parent) can leave a class from the Me tab at any time, which stops sharing and deletes the student's class record. To ask us to delete class data, email ali.houmani@exprealty.com.

Advertising

Maple Leap contains no advertising and no third-party ad networks.

Children

We comply with applicable children's privacy laws, including COPPA in the United States and PIPEDA in Canada. Because we do not collect personal information from children, there is nothing to share, sell or disclose.

Website

The maplehilllearning.com website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log standard technical information such as IP addresses when pages are loaded, under its own privacy policy.

Changes and contact

If we change this policy we will update the date above. Questions: alihoumani6@gmail.com.
